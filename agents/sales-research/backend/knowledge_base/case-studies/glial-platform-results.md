## Lazarus Effect — Lost Deal Revival

The HubSpot Sync Agent monitors "Closed Lost" deals for new positive context:
- New funding round at prospect's company
- Leadership change (new VP Sales hired)
- Competitor they chose closes down or gets bad press
- Prospect posts about pain with their current solution

When detected: Glial automatically surfaces the deal in the Command Center with a "Revival Signal" tag and generates a new outreach with the updated context.

**Expected Revival Rate**: 5–15% of Closed Lost deals have a "Why Now" trigger within 12 months.

## Sakshi SDR Workflow Results

**Context**: Sakshi is a channel partner SDR doing outbound outreach using Glial-generated signals.

### Daily Targets (2 hours/day)
- Reports reviewed: 8–10
- LinkedIn connection requests sent: 15/day max
- Follow-up emails: 5–10/day
- Weekly outreach touchpoints: 15+ (LinkedIn + email + follow-ups)

### Workflow Efficiency
- Time from signal detection to personalized outreach: < 5 minutes (vs. 30+ min manual)
- Message rewrite rate: < 20% of Glial's generated hook (high fidelity = better performance)
- Positive reply → immediate handoff to Harish for discovery call

### Key Observations
- Competitor intercept signals produce highest-quality leads (score 55–75 range)
- Inbound signals (form fill, demo request) produce highest scores (70–90 range)
- Keyword search leads require more qualification but produce volume
- Best performing angle: "Competitor Blind Spot Hook" when referencing exact post/comment
