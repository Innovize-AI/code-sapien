# AI Strategy Session SOP — Innovize AI
Version: 3.0 | Owner: Discovery Lead / Tech Lead

## Purpose
This SOP guides Discovery Lead through conducting the Free AI Strategy Session — Step 1 of Innovize AI's 3-step engagement funnel. The session is a validation and readiness check, not a sales call.

Output: A clear **Go / No-Go decision + Remediation Plan** for the prospect.

---

## What This Session Is (and Isn't)

| This IS | This is NOT |
|---------|------------|
| A readiness assessment | A product demo |
| A diagnostic conversation | A pitch |
| A trust-building moment | A commitment request |
| A Go/No-Go decision | A proposal session |

If they aren't ready, tell them. That's what builds trust.

---

## The 7 Readiness Dimensions

| Dimension | What We're Assessing |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **KPI Clarity** | Can they define success in measurable terms? |
| **Data Readiness (CLEAN)** | Is their data accessible, clean, and structured enough for AI? |
| **Realistic Expectations** | Do they accept 85% accuracy + human review, or demand perfection? |
| **HITL Design** | Is there a human reviewer identified for high-stakes decisions? |
| **Change Management (TRUST)** | Can they drive adoption? Do they have an executive sponsor? |
| **Compliance & Security (GUARD)** | Are there HIPAA, GDPR, SOX, or data residency requirements? |
| **Execution Fundamentals** | Is ONE workflow scoped? Is there an internal project owner? |

## Session Structure (60–75 min)

### Step 1 — Organisational Snapshot (10 min)
Goal: Understand the business, team, and current state.

Questions:
- Walk me through your business — what do you do, who are your customers, and what does your team look like?
- What does a typical week look like for your ops/sales/support team?
- What tools are you currently using? (CRM, helpdesk, analytics)
- Have you tried any AI or automation tools before — Zapier, ChatGPT, anything? What happened?
- What's the one thing eating the most time right now?

### Step 2 — Business Goals & KPI Discovery (10 min)
Framework: **SMART-AI**

Questions:
- What does a good month look like vs. a bad one?
- Which part of the business are you most worried about right now?
- If this problem was completely solved in 90 days — what would be different?
- Who feels this pain the most — you, your team, your customers?
- What's the business impact of the current problem — time, money, errors, missed growth?

SMART-AI Criteria (Tech Lead builds KPIs from notes):
- **Specific** — what exact business outcome needs to move?
- **Measurable** — what baseline can we start from?
- **Achievable** — what's realistic with current AI?
- **Relevant** — does this metric actually matter to the business?
- **Time-bound** — 30/60/90 day milestone?
- **Aligned** — who needs to agree this equals success?
- **Iterative** — improve over time vs. demanding perfection at launch?

### Step 3 — Data & Workflow Discovery (15 min)
Framework: **CLEAN**

Data surface questions:
- Where does most of your business data live — systems, files, emails, or people's heads?
- Who owns the data and can you access it freely?
- Is data updated in real-time, daily, or manually?

Per-Workflow Template (repeat for top 2–3 workflows):
- What triggers this process?
- Who does it and how? (manual steps, tools involved)
- How long does it take per instance? Per week total?
- What data is created or used?
- Where does it live — one system or scattered?
- Who currently makes the final call in this process?
- What's the cost of a wrong decision?

CLEAN Data Quality Check:
- **C**omplete — are required fields populated?
- **L**egible — is the format consistent?
- **E**xportable — can they get it out of their system?
- **A**ccessible — is there a data owner who can share it?
- **N**ormalised — is it structured, or free-form/unstructured?

### Step 4 — Expectation Setting (10 min)
- "AI reaches 85–90% accuracy in most production systems. The remaining 10–15% requires a human reviewer to catch edge cases. Is your team comfortable with that model?"
- "The goal is not to eliminate humans — it's to 10× their output. Every high-stakes decision still has a human in the loop."
- Watch for: demanding 100% accuracy → flag as high-risk; accepting HITL model → green light

### Step 5 — Change Management (TRUST) (10 min)
- Who would be the internal champion driving adoption?
- What does a typical change/rollout look like in your company?
- Are end users aware you're exploring this? What's their likely reaction?
- Do you have an executive sponsor (CEO/CTO/COO) who would champion this?

### Step 6 — Compliance & Security (GUARD) (5 min)
- Is your industry regulated? (Healthcare → HIPAA, Finance → SOX, EU data → GDPR)
- Do you handle PII or sensitive customer data?
- Do you have a compliance or data governance owner?
- Will IT or Legal need to sign off?

### Step 7 — Execution Fundamentals (5 min)
- Have you picked ONE workflow to start with, or are you still deciding?
- Who would be the internal project owner — someone who can give us 2–4 hours/week?
- Do you have bandwidth to run a pilot in the next 30–60 days?

## Go / No-Go Decision Criteria

### ✅ GO — Proceed to Pilot
- Clear pain point with measurable business impact
- At least one workflow scoped
- Data exists (even if messy — we can work with it)
- Decision maker on the call or accessible
- Accepts HITL model
- Budget signal ($5K–$10K range confirmed or implied)

### 🚫 NO-GO — Remediation Plan Instead
- Can't articulate any specific pain or goal
- Data doesn't exist or is completely inaccessible
- Demands 100% accuracy ("I need it to be perfect")
- No internal champion or executive sponsor
- Budget clearly not there (< $3K)
- Compliance requirements they haven't thought through

### Remediation Plan (for No-Go)
Tell them honestly: "Based on what we've discussed, I don't think you're ready for a full AI deployment yet. Here's why, and here's what I'd suggest doing first: [specific 2–3 actions]. Come back in 60–90 days and we'll reassess."
