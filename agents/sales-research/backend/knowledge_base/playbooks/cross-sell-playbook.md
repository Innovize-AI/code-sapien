## The 6-Week Cross-Sell Timeline

| Week | Phase | Your Actions | What to Listen For |
|------|-------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1–2 | Glial Deployment | Kickoff, ICP config, CRM integration, Slack setup | "Our ops team spends hours on X," "We manually do Y" |
| 3–4 | Glial Live | Share quick wins via Slack. Build credibility. | Specific workflows: "Contract review," "Lead enrichment," "Data entry" |
| **5** | **Casual Mention** | During check-in: "By the way, we also help companies automate [X]..." | "Oh really? Tell me more" vs. "Not right now" |
| **6** | **Free Assessment** | Deliver 1-page: Current state → Automated state → ROI → Next step | "Let's move forward" vs. "Not the right time" |

**Key Principle**: Do NOT pitch AI transformation during kickoff. Listen and document in Weeks 1–4. Mention too early = feels pushy.

## Qualification Criteria

### ✅ QUALIFY — Pursue the Cross-Sell
- Mentioned specific manual workflows (contracts, data entry, research, reporting)
- Team size 10+ people (scale makes automation worth it)
- Budget authority confirmed ($10K+ range)
- Happy with Glial (actively using, clicking Slack alerts)
- Decision maker accessible (CEO/CTO/COO)

### ❌ DISQUALIFY — Don't Pursue
- Small team (< 5 people) — hire a VA instead
- Vague complaints with no concrete process ("we need to be more efficient")
- Budget constrained (barely afforded Glial)
- Not using Glial actively
- Already building in-house AI capabilities

## Week 5: The Casual Mention Script

**Setup**: Start the call with Glial updates. Build goodwill first. Then, last 2–3 minutes:

> "By the way, [Name], I wanted to mention — we also help companies like yours automate [specific pain point you documented]. I remember you mentioned [exact phrase from Week 2: 'your reps spend 2 hours/day researching accounts']. We built a system for another B2B SaaS client that auto-generates pre-call briefings from LinkedIn, company data, and recent news. Reduced their research time from 2 hours to 15 minutes per rep. Would something like that be helpful for your team?"

**Possible Responses:**

| Their Response | Your Next Step |
|----------------|---------------|
| "Oh really? Tell me more." | GREEN LIGHT. "Let me put together a quick assessment showing what this would look like for your team. No charge, just want to show you what's possible." |
| "Maybe down the road, not right now." | YELLOW LIGHT. "No problem! If things change in Q2, let me know." Revisit in 3 months. |
| "We're building that in-house." | RED LIGHT. "Got it, makes sense." Don't pursue further. |

---

## Week 6: Free Assessment Structure (1 Page)

**Section 1: Current State**
Restate their pain in concrete terms: "Your 8 SDRs spend ~90 minutes/day (12 hours/week total) manually researching accounts. This is 624 hours/year of non-selling activity."

**Section 2: Automated State**
Describe the solution: "We build an AI research agent that pulls data from LinkedIn, Crunchbase, company websites, and recent news. Every morning, your SDRs receive a pre-call briefing with company overview, funding/news, tech stack, pain points, and suggested talking points. Research time drops to 15 minutes/rep."

**Section 3: ROI Projection**
"12 hours/week saved × $30/hour (loaded cost) = $360/week = $18,720/year in reclaimed time. If even 20% converts to extra prospecting, that's 2–3 additional meetings/month."

**Section 4: Next Step**
"We can build and deploy this in 5–7 weeks. Investment: $7,500 one-time build + optional $1,500/quarter for optimization."
