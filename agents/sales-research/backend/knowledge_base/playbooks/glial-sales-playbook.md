# Glial Sales Playbook — ICP, Demo, Objections & Qualification

## Overview
This playbook guides the sales team through qualifying, demoing, and closing Glial Revenue Intelligence deployments.

---

## The Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary Personas
| Persona | Why They Buy | Key Pain |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Founders (Seed–Series A B2B SaaS) | Still selling, exhausted. Need leverage without hiring. | No time for research, every deal matters. |
| VP of Sales / Head of Sales (3–20 reps) | Obsessed with efficiency and pipeline quality. | Reps spend 4+ hrs/day on research instead of selling. |
| RevOps Leaders | Love tools and automation. Easiest to convince on tech value. | Manual workflows, tool fatigue, no single source of truth. |

### Company Sweet Spot
- **Sector**: B2B Tech / SaaS — they understand software value
- **ACV**: $10K+ — high-ticket sales justifies deep research per lead
- **Team Size**: 3–20 sales reps — scale-up phase is the sweet spot
- **Geography (Tier 1)**: US, UK, Canada, Australia
- **Funding**: Seed to Series B — they have budget, not enterprise procurement

## Qualification — Green Lights & Red Flags

### ✅ GREEN LIGHTS — Qualify immediately
- "We are hiring SDRs / BDRs" → automation play over headcount
- "Our reply rates are tanking" → need better quality/personalization
- "We use Apollo/ZoomInfo but the data is cold" → understand static data problem
- "My team spends too much time on admin / research" → Glial removes that
- "We're struggling with tool fatigue — too many platforms" → Glial consolidates to Slack
- Complaining about Clay.com, Apollo, ZoomInfo on LinkedIn posts → in-market buyer

### 🚩 RED FLAGS — Walk away
- "We just need 10,000 leads for a blast" → wants volume spam, not intelligence
- "We sell to consumers (B2C)" → Glial is optimized for B2B LinkedIn signals
- "We have zero budget for sales tools" → premium infrastructure play
- "We don't do outbound / 100% PLG" → no use case yet
- Team size < 3 reps → not enough pain to justify deployment cost

### ❓ The 3 Magic Qualification Questions
1. "How much time does your team spend researching a prospect before they send an email?" → Answer > 15 mins? **PERFECT MATCH**
2. "Are you focused on volume (spray) or quality (strategic) right now?" → Answer: Quality? **PERFECT MATCH**
3. "If you could clone your best sales rep's brain, would you?" → Answer: Yes? **PERFECT MATCH** — that's literally what Glial does.

## Demo Script — 15–20 Min Validation Call

### 1. Introduction & Context (2 min)
- "I'm validating Glial — a Revenue Intelligence platform built for lean sales teams who can't afford the 4-hour manual research grind."
- "I'm looking for feedback from people like you who are actually in the trenches of scaling GTM."

### 2. Problem Discovery (5 min)
- "How does your team currently prepare for a high-priority outreach?"
- "Where do you see the biggest bottleneck: Finding the lead, or finding the 'Why Now' signal?"
- "What happens when a prospect engages with a competitor's post — does your team know about it?"

### 3. The Glial Demo — Value Props (7 min)
- **Competitor Tracking**: "We harvested your interaction on [Competitor]'s post to find you. We do that at scale for your rivals."
- **Executive Synthesis**: Show the 1-page CSO briefing. "This connects hiring cues and news into a narrative so your reps sound like experts."
- **Pain Discovery**: "We found your team is currently hiring for [Role]. That usually means a bottleneck in [System]. Does that resonate?"
- **Slack Alert**: "Instead of logging into another tool, your rep gets this in Slack with an 'Analyze & Draft' button."

### 4. Feedback & Pricing Discovery (5 min)
- "Of what you saw, what would have the most impact on your team's output this week?"
- "What would a tool like this need to do for you to consider it a must-have for your stack?"
- "If we offered a pilot where we handled research for your top 50 accounts, would $3.5K–$5K be a reasonable starting point?"

### 5. Next Steps
- "Can I send you the Executive Briefing for your top 5 accounts to review?"
- "Would you be open to a 2nd chat once we've integrated your feedback?"

## Daily Sales Workflow (SDR/Partner)

### Block 1 — Signal Review & Research (45 min)
1. Check #glial-signals Slack channel — look for 🔥 Potential Opportunity alerts
2. Triage Identified Profiles in Glial — review new autopilot-surfaced leads
3. Trigger research on 3–4 qualified leads simultaneously
4. Read each report fully — **always read CSO Verdict first**
5. Decide: Glial pitch or AI Services pitch? (see ICP above)

### Block 2 — Outreach Execution (45 min)
1. Engage with lead's posts BEFORE sending connection request (comment/like first)
2. Send LinkedIn connection requests using Glial-generated hook — max 15/day
3. Send follow-up emails to leads who accepted but haven't replied (3+ days)

### Block 3 — Follow-ups & Logging (30 min)
1. Check for replies — positive reply = immediately hand off to Harish
2. Log ALL outreach with date, lead URL, angle used, draft fidelity %, response
3. Monday only: review which keywords/competitors produced leads this week
