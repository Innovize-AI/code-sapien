# Glial Advanced Demo Script: The Revenue Intelligence Factory

## Part 1: Strategic Discovery (3 Mins)
- **Opening**: "We're validating Glial—a Revenue Intelligence platform that architects the narrative to win high-stakes accounts. Most tools just find leads; we architect the win."
- **The Wedge**: "I noticed you were evaluating [Competitor/Signal]. Most people find the tool fatigue and the raw data overhead of those platforms overwhelming. Does that sit with your team currently?"
- **The Shift**: "Glial is different. It’s not a SaaS you rent; it’s a **Private Asset (PaaS)** you own. We build the engine inside your infra, and you pay raw API costs."

## Part 2: The Command Center Walkthrough (7 Mins)

### Module A: The Identify & Intercept (Signals)
- **Showcase**: The **Command Center** dashboard.
- **Narrative**: "Here is your 24/7 Scout. It’s currently monitoring [Competitor X] and keywords like '[Keyword]'. The moment an ICP prospect engages, they hit this queue."
- **Key Line**: "We intercept the buyer *during* the decision window."

### Module B: Primary Evaluation & Sifting
- **Showcase**: The auto-tagging logic (Fit vs. Decision Maker).
- **Narrative**: "Glial has already done the first pass. It auto-filters competitors and high-grades founders and VPs so your team only sees the gold."

### Module C: Deep Analysis & Strategic Command
- **Showcase**: Triggering a "Deep Analysis" from the Command Center.
- **Narrative**: "This is the **Sales Brain** in action. It ingests your specific playbooks and ROI stories to build a custom briefing. It doesn't just say 'they need AI'—it tells you *why now* and gives you the exact tactical hook."

### Module D: The Continuous Loop (Slack)
- **Showcase**: Slack Notification showing a signal update.
- **Narrative**: "Reps live in Slack. The moment a prospect downloads your whitepaper or comments on another post, Glial re-runs the analysis and updates the 'Next Best Action' in real-time."
- **Key Line**: "Your intelligence is never stale. It evolves with the lead."

## Part 3: The Architecture of Ownership (3 Mins)
- **PaaS Focus**: "Since you own the infrastructure, there's zero markup on API costs. We provide **Neural Tuning** monthly to ensure the AI's reasoning stays aligned with your closed-won deals."
- **The ROI**: "A 20-person sales team's research capability for the cost of one entry-level SDR."

## Part 4: Feedback & Close (2 Mins)
- **The Ask**: "We're looking for 2-3 strategic partners to join our Q1 cohort. Based on what you've seen, does this infrastructure ownership model solve the tool-fatigue problem for you?"
