# Discovery Questionnaire: Glial (Sales-Research)

This questionnaire is designed to qualify leads for the Glial platform, specifically targeting B2B SaaS Founders and Sales Leaders.

## 1. Firmographics & "Ideal Fit" Check
*Goal: Ensure the prospect has the scale and structure to benefit from Glial.*

- **Company Size:** Are you currently between 30 and 200 employees? (Sweet spot is Seed to Series B).
- **Sales Team Structure:** How many SDRs/BDRs and AEs do you currently have on the team?
- **Current CRM:** Are you using HubSpot or Salesforce to manage your sales pipeline?
- **Outbound Volume:** On average, how many cold outreaches (email/LinkedIn) is your team sending per week?

## 2. Current Workflow & "Grunt Work" Analysis
*Goal: Identify the manual bottlenecks and the "Cost of Research."*

- **Research Time:** How much time does an average SDR spend researching a single account before hitting "send"?
- **The Hook:** How is your team currently generating "personalized hooks"? Is it manual browsing of LinkedIn/News, or do you use a tool?
- **Data Freshness:** How often do you find that the information in your current database (Apollo/ZoomInfo) is outdated or "static"?
- **Ramp Time:** For a new SDR hire, how long does it take (in weeks) before they are consistently finding 10+ qualified opportunities on their own?

## 3. The "Signal Gap" (Competitor & Intent Tracking)
*Goal: Uncover missed opportunities that Glial's "Listening Post" would catch.*

- **Competitor Monitoring:** When a qualified buyer comments on a competitor's (e.g., Apollo, ZoomInfo, Clay) LinkedIn post with a complaint or a question, does your team hear about it?
- **The "Lazarus Effect":** Do you have a process for automatically "reviving" Closed-Lost deals when a key stakeholder changes jobs or they receive new funding?
- **Social Intent:** Is your team actively monitoring LinkedIn keywords like "Hiring SDRs" or "Outbound fatigue" to intercept prospects at the exact moment of pain?

## 4. Tool Stack & "Software Fatigue"
*Goal: Pivot to the "Private Engine" ownership model vs. renting software.*

- **Tool Count:** How many different tools does your team log into daily just to execute outbound (e.g., Apollo for data, Clay for enrichment, Salesloft for sending, LinkedIn for research)?
- **Data Costs:** Are you frustrated with paying markups on API data (credits) instead of paying raw costs for what you actually use?
- **Ownership:** Would there be a strategic advantage for your company to **own** its own revenue engine and keep the data within your own infrastructure?

## 5. Motivation & Next Steps
- **Success Metric:** If we could improve your reply rates by 10-15% by delivering "warm intent" leads directly to Slack, what would that do for your Q3/Q4 revenue targets?
