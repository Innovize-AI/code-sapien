| Why This Works: Trust already established: Client has seen you deliver (Glial deployed successfully) You're already inside: Weekly Slack updates give you visibility into their operations Natural discovery: Glial implementation reveals other pain points organically No cold outreach needed: Warm conversation, not a sales pitch Higher close rate: 60-80% vs 30-40% on cold AI transformation deals |
| :---- |

# **Step-by-Step Crosssell Playbook**

## **Week 1-2 (Glial Deployment)**

| Action | Details |
| :---- | :---- |
| Discovery Questions | During Glial kickoff, ask about broader operations: "What manual processes eat the most time for your sales team?" "What data do reps need to look up manually before calls?" "Where does your ops team spend time on repetitive tasks?" |
| Document Patterns | Keep a running list of pain points mentioned casually: "We have 100 contracts to review each month", "Our customer support team copies data between systems", "We manually enrich leads from webinar signups" |

## **Week 3-4 (Glial Live, Early Results)**

| Action | Details |
| :---- | :---- |
| Share Quick Win | Slack message: "Glial found 3 warm leads this week that matched your ICP perfectly. Here's the breakdown..." Build credibility and trust. |
| Casual Mention | During weekly check-in: "By the way, we also help companies automate \[specific pain point they mentioned\]. Saw you dealing with \[X\], would that be helpful?" Keep it light, not a pitch. |

## **Week 5-6 (Formal Opportunity Assessment)**

| Action | Details |
| :---- | :---- |
| Qualify Interest | If they express interest: "Would it be helpful if I put together a quick assessment of what automation would look like for \[specific workflow\]? No charge, just want to show you what's possible." |
| Free Assessment | Deliver a 1-page assessment: Current state → Automated state → ROI projection → Next step (pilot). This is essentially a mini-proposal that seeds the conversation. |
