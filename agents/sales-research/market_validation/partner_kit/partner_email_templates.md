# Partner Outreach Templates

Use these templates to introduce Glial to your network. Feel free to tweak the tone to match your relationship with the person.

---

## Template A: The "Inner Circle" Text/DM
**Best for:** Close contacts, friends, or peers you talk to regularly.
**Channel:** WhatsApp, iMessage, LinkedIn DM.

> "Hey [Name], quick question – are you guys still doing all your sales research manually? I just saw a demo of this new tool called Glial that automates the whole 'deep dive' process before outreach. It’s pretty wild, creates a full briefing doc for every lead.
> 
> I know the founder and they’re opening up a few spots for their pilot. Thought of you/your team immediately. Want me to intro you?"

---

## Template B: The "Value Add" Email
**Best for:** Portfolio companies, clients, or professional acquaintances.
**Subject:** Tool recommendation for [Company Name]'s sales team

> Hi [Name],
> 
> I was thinking about our conversation regarding [scaling the sales team / improving efficiency / SDR bottlenecks] and wanted to share a resource I’ve come across.
> 
> It’s a system called **Glial Revenue Intelligence**. Essentially, it builds an automated "research analyst" for your sales team. Instead of reps spending hours on LinkedIn trying to find "why now" triggers, this system monitors your market and hands them a 1-page briefing on exactly who to contact and why.
> 
> It’s different from things like Apollo/ZoomInfo because it focuses on the *context* (buying signals), not just the contact info.
> 
> I have a direct line to their team. If you're interested, I can make a warm intro so you can bypass the waitlist and see if it’s a fit.
> 
> Best,
> [Your Name]

---

## Template C: The "Problem Solver" (Specific Pain Point)
**Best for:** When you hear someone complain about "spammy emails" or "hiring difficulties."
**Subject:** Fixing the [hiring/research] bottleneck

> Hi [Name],
> 
> You mentioned the other day that [hiring good SDRs is tough / your team is burning out on lead gen].
> 
> Have you looked at automating the research piece? I’ve been recommending **Glial** to a few folks. It effectively replaces the manual grunt work of an SDR (finding signals, checking news, writing hooks) so your actual humans can focus on closing deals.
> 
> One of my other contacts mentioned it saved their team about 20 hours a week per rep.
> 
> Happy to connect you with them if you want to see how it works. No pressure, just thought it might solve that specific headache you mentioned.
> 
> Cheers,
> [Your Name]
