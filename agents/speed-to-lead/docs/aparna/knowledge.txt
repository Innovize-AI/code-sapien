## PRICING GUIDANCE

Aparna CyberZed (only project with published price):
- 3 BHK 1471 sq ft: Rs 7,999 x 1471 = approx Rs 1.18 Cr (base)
- 3 BHK 2001 sq ft: Rs 7,999 x 2001 = approx Rs 1.60 Cr (base)
- Registration and stamp duty: approximately 7.5% of sale value additional
- GST: 5% on under-construction properties
- Maintenance deposit and corpus fund: as per actual

For Moonstone, Newlands, Synergy: pricing shared on request after site visit — Aparna policy is to quote final price post-site visit to allow customisation basis unit selection and floor premium.

Floor premium: Higher floors command premium of Rs 50–150 per sq ft depending on project and view.
Corner units: Typically Rs 75–100 per sq ft premium.
Payment plans: Construction-linked plan (most common), 20:80 plan, and subvention schemes available depending on the project. Speak with our sales team for the latest scheme.

Payment plan details:
- Construction-linked plan (CLP): You pay in tranches tied to construction milestones (foundation, slab, floor-wise completion, possession). Most common and most transparent — you pay as progress happens.
- 20:80 plan: Pay 20% now (down payment), then 80% at possession. Suitable if you want minimal outflow during construction. Builder arranges bank loan disbursement at possession stage.
- Subvention scheme: You pay the down payment (typically 10–20%), and the builder pays the EMI on your home loan for the construction period. Your EMI starts only after possession. This reduces your monthly burden during the construction phase significantly. Subvention availability varies by project and bank tie-up — confirm with sales for current scheme.

## FAQS

Q: Are all Aparna projects RERA registered?
A: Yes. Every active project is RERA registered. RERA numbers are published on our website and verifiable at rera.telangana.gov.in.

Q: What is the minimum investment?
A: The entry point for Aparna projects is approximately Rs 90 Lakhs for a 2 BHK at Aparna Synergy (Gandi Maisamma). For premium projects like Moonstone, pricing starts above Rs 2 Cr. Our portfolio is suited for buyers with a budget of Rs 90 Lakhs and above. Budgets below Rs 80 Lakhs are unlikely to find a fit in our current portfolio.

Q: Do you arrange home loans?
A: Yes. Aparna has tie-ups with all major banks — SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak. Our team facilitates loan approvals and can connect you with relationship managers for the best rates.

Q: Can NRIs book Aparna flats?
A: Yes. NRIs can book freely under FEMA guidelines. Power of attorney arrangements and NRE/NRO account payments are all facilitated. Dedicated NRI desk available.

Q: What is the typical possession timeline?
A: Under-construction projects are 3–4 years from launch to possession. RERA timelines are committed and penalties apply for delays — giving buyers legal protection.

Project-wise possession estimates:
- Aparna CyberZed (new launch): Expected possession approximately 2028–2029. RERA number P01100011037 — exact RERA-committed date visible on rera.telangana.gov.in.
- Aparna Moonstone (under construction): Expected possession approximately 2027. RERA: P02400009478.
- Aparna Newlands (under construction): Expected possession approximately 2027. RERA: P01100007480.
- Aparna Synergy (under construction): Expected possession approximately 2027–2028. RERA: P02200006615.
Note: All RERA-registered projects carry legal penalties for delays at 10.75% per annum — buyer interests are protected.

Q: Is there a maintenance charge after possession?
A: Yes. Aparna Property Management Services (APMS) manages the community after possession. Maintenance charges are typically Rs 2–4 per sq ft per month depending on the project.

Q: Can I visit the project without a cab?
A: Absolutely. You can drive yourself. Parking is available at all project sites. However the cab is a complimentary convenience — many buyers prefer it.

Q: What happens if the project is delayed?
A: RERA mandates compensation at 10.75% per year (SBI lending rate) for delays. Aparna has a strong track record of on-time delivery — Aparna Sarovar Zenith (2022) and Aparna Westside (2019) were both delivered on schedule.

Q: Can I customise my flat interiors before possession?
A: Structural changes are not permitted post-slab. However dry works — flooring upgrades, modular kitchen selection, paint colours, and bathroom fixtures — can be customised through Aparna's interiors team at an additional cost.

Q: What documents do I need for booking?
A: Aadhar card, PAN card, passport-size photo, and cheque/RTGS for EOI amount. For home loan buyers, last 6 months salary slips and 2 years ITR.
