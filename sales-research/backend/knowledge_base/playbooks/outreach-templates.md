# Glial Multi-Angle Outreach Templates

## Philosophy
Don't use a single message for everyone. Match the outreach angle to the signal Glial detected. Test systematically and double down on winners.

---

## A/B Testing Framework

Run 50 prospects per angle for 4 weeks. Track:
- Open rate
- Reply rate (target: 15%+)
- Demo request rate
- Objection type

After Week 4: keep top 2 angles, drop the rest. Iterate on objections.

---

## 3-Touch Email Sequence (After LinkedIn Connection Accepted)

**Touch 1 (Day 1 of connection)**: Send the primary angle email above.

**Touch 2 (Day 5, no reply)**: Short follow-up.
> Subject: Re: [original subject]
> Hi [Name], just wanted to bump this up. Did the idea of [specific value prop] resonate at all? Happy to keep it to 15 minutes.

**Touch 3 (Day 10, no reply)**: Value drop + soft close.
> Subject: One last thing, [Name]
> Sharing something useful regardless: [1 insight from Glial's monitoring data, e.g., "we're seeing a lot of VP Sales at B2B SaaS companies mentioning tool fatigue this month"]. If timing ever works out, I'd love to show you how we identify signals like this at scale. No pressure either way.
