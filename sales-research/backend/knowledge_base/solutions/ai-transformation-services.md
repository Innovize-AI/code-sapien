## Service Categories

### 1. Sales & Revenue Intelligence
Always-on research, signal detection, personalization at scale.
- **Example deliverables**: Lead enrichment, timing optimization, next-best-action briefings, pre-call research automation
- **Price range**: $5K – $8K
- **Primary product**: Glial Research Platform

### 2. Document Intelligence
Extract structured data from PDFs, contracts, invoices. Vector-based retrieval (RAG) for intelligent Q&A over document libraries.
- **Example deliverables**: Contract analysis, data entry automation, invoice processing, compliance document review
- **Price range**: $7K – $10K
- **Key differentiator**: HIPAA-compliant, self-hosted, full audit trails

### 3. Inbox & Workflow Automation
Intent detection, smart routing, suggested replies. CRM enrichment and action triggers based on email content.
- **Example deliverables**: Support ticket triage, inquiry automation, email-to-CRM sync, smart follow-up routing
- **Price range**: $6K – $9K

### 4. Reporting Automation
Ingest messy CSV/Excel data, generate charts, produce narrative summaries. Scheduled reporting that runs itself.
- **Example deliverables**: Weekly KPI decks, board reporting automation, dashboard generation, data pipeline builds
- **Price range**: $4K – $6K

### 5. Lead Enrichment Pipelines
Scoring and routing leads from signups, forms, and webinars.
- **Example deliverables**: Auto-score inbound leads, enrich with firmographic data, route to right rep/sequence
- **Price range**: $5K – $7K

### 6. Compliant AI Systems
Audit trails, data residency, and HITL for HIPAA/SOC2 requirements.
- **Example deliverables**: Self-hosted AI with compliance controls, manual override on every decision, PII handling
- **Price range**: $8K – $12K
