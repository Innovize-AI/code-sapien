### **The Solution: Signal-Driven Intelligence**

#### **1. The Command Center (The Steering Wheel)**
A dedicated frontend for leadership to steer the "hunt" in real-time. 
*   **Identify & Intercept:** Automatically find prospects commenting on competitor posts or discussing specific pain points via LinkedIn keywords.
*   **Primary Evaluation:** Leads are instantly tagged as ✅ **Potential Fit** or 👤 **Decision Maker** based on your specific ICP criteria.

#### **2. Invisible Workflow (The Slack-First Motion)**
Your reps never have to leave Slack. Glial brings the intelligence to where they work.
*   **Instant Alerts:** Get notified the second a high-signal prospect is identified.
*   **Remote Action Buttons:** Click **[Analyze Prospect]** or **[Push to CRM]** directly from the Slack notification.
*   **Decision Briefings:** Receive **High-Stakes Intelligence Reports** (Executive synthesis, pain point hypotheses, and 5-step tactical playbooks) as a thread or PDF.

#### **3. The Continuous Signal Loop (Always-On)**
Once an account is monitored, Glial tracks every new interaction (emails, lead magnet downloads, competitor comments, or new meetings).
*   **Evolving Advice:** When a signal changes, the system **re-runs the analysis.**
*   **Real-Time Updates:** Reps receive updated "Next Steps" in Slack as the prospect's context shifts.

#### **4. Customized Knowledge Base (The Memory)**
A secure, private repository where the AI is trained on your specific solutions, case studies, and proprietary sales playbooks. The "Brain" doesn't just know the web; it knows **your** business.
