### **The System: How Glial Works**

#### **1. The Command Center (Identify & Intercept)**
Your team gets a dedicated frontend to steer the engine. The system acts as a 24/7 scout, identifying prospects from:
*   **Competitor Intercepts**: Buyers engaging with competitor posts on LinkedIn.
*   **Keyword Intelligence**: Public posts discussing specific pain points or budget triggers.
*   **Apollo Integration**: High-fidelity lead sourcing mapped to your custom filters.

#### **2. Primary Evaluation (Instant Sifting)**
Before a rep touches a lead, Glial performs a primary evaluation based on LinkedIn profiles. Leads are instantly tagged:
*   ✅ **Potential Fit** | 👤 **Decision Maker** | 🚫 **Competitor** (Auto-filtered)

#### **3. High-Stakes Deep Analysis (Decision Briefings)**
Reps trigger a **Deep Analysis** in the Command Center. The system ingests your unique **Sales Playbooks** to generate:
*   **Why Now**: The specific trigger (e.g., a post about unmanaged cloud costs).
*   **Pain Point Hypothesis**: AI-driven analysis of likely blockers.
*   **Strategic Command**: Hyper-personalized hooks and a 5-step tactical engagement plan.

#### **4. The Continuous Signal Loop (Always-Monitoring)**
Once an account is monitored, Glial tracks new signals (emails, downloads, competitor comments).
*   **Evolving Advice**: When a signal changes, the system **re-runs the analysis**.
*   **Slack Updates**: Reps receive updated "Next Steps" the moment the context shifts.
