# Demo Script for Glial Market Validation Call (v1)

*Goal: 15-20 Min Problem & Value Validation Call*

## 1. Introduction & Context (2 Mins)
- "I'm validating Glial—a Revenue Intelligence platform built for lean sales teams who can't afford the 4-hour manual research grind."
- "I'm looking for feedback from people like you who are actually in the trenches of scaling GTM."

## 2. Problem Discovery (5 Mins)
- "How does your team currently prepare for a high-priority outreach?"
- "Where do you see the biggest bottleneck: Finding the lead, or finding the 'Why Now' signal?"

## 3. The Glial Demo (Value Props) (7 Mins)
- **Competitor Tracking**: "We harvested your interaction on {{Competitor}}'s post to find you. We do that at scale for your rivals."
- **Executive Synthesis**: Show the 1-page summary. "This connects hiring cues and news into a narrative so your reps sound like experts."
- **Pain Discovery**: "We found that your team is currently hiring for {{Role}}. That usually means a bottleneck in {{System}}. Does that resonate?"

## 4. Feedback & Pricing Discovery (5 Mins)
- "Of what you saw, what would have the most impact on your team's output this week?"
- "What would a tool like this need to do for you to consider it a 'must-have' for your stack?"
- "If we offered a pilot where we handled research for your top 50 accounts, would $X be a reasonable starting point?"

## 5. Next Steps
- "Can I send you the Executive Briefing for your top 5 accounts to review?"
- "Would you be open to a 2nd chat once we've integrated your feedback?"
