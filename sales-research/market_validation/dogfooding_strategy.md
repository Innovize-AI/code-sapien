# Glial Dogfooding Strategy: "The Cobbler's Shoes"

**Objective:** Use Glial (our own product) to find 20 high-fit beta customers for Glial.

We will configure our own "Command Center" to target **Founders, VPs of Sales, and RevOps leaders** at B2B SaaS companies (Seed - Series B).

---

## 1. Competitor Intercepts (The "Steal" Strategy)
*Goal: Find users dissatisfied with or asking questions about our competitors.*

**Who we will monitor (The "Big 3"):**
1.  **Apollo.io:** Look for users complaining about "bad data," "spam filters," or "support."
2.  **Clay.com:** Look for users saying it's "too complicated," "expensive," or asking for "experts/agencies" (perfect for our managed service angle).
3.  **ZoomInfo:** Look for "alternatives," "contracts being too expensive," or "data decay."

**Action:**
*   **Trigger:** User comments on a Competitor's LinkedIn post (or a post mentioning them).
*   **Glial Playbook:**
    *   **Analyze:** Is this a user complaint or a fan?
    *   **Hook:** "Saw you were struggling with Clay's complexity. We built a 'Done-For-You' engine that handles that..."

## 2. Keyword Intelligence (The "Pain" Radar)
*Goal: Intercept conversations about the problems we solve.*

**Keywords/Phrases to Monitor:**
*   "Hiring SDRs" / "Hiring BDRs" (They are trying to solve the problem with bodies).
*   "Cold email open rates" / "Reply rates" (They are failing at outbound).
*   "Sales intelligence tools" / "Sales stack" (They are shopping).
*   "Manual research" / "Sales research time" (Direct pain point).
*   "Personalization at scale" (The exact promise of Glial).

**Action:**
*   **Trigger:** Founder/Sales Leader uses these keywords in a post.
*   **Glial Playbook:**
    *   **Analyze:** Are they asking for advice?
    *   **Hook:** "You mentioned hiring SDRs. Have you considered automating the research part first so your new hires don't burn out?"

## 3. Hiring Signals (The "Budget" Indicator)
*Goal: Identify companies spending money on Sales Growth.*

**Roles to Watch For:**
*   **"Founding SDR"** (Founder is stepping back, needs process).
*   **"Head of Sales" / "VP of Sales"** (New leader = new tools budget).
*   **"RevOps Manager"** (They are hired to fix efficiency).

**Action:**
*   **Trigger:** Company posts a job opening for these roles.
*   **Glial Playbook:**
    *   **Enrich:** Find the Founder or CEO.
    *   **Hook:** "Congrats on the new Head of Sales search. Usually, that means you're ramping up outbound..."

## 4. Inbound "Waitlist" Enrichment
*Goal: Turn passive signups into active beta users.*

**Source:** Our own Waitlist / Newsletter / Lead Magnets.

**Action:**
*   **Trigger:** New email added to database.
*   **Glial Playbook:**
    *   **Instant Enrich:** Who is this? (Role, Company, Funding).
    *   **Filter:**
        *   **Tier 1 (Fit):** Founder/Sales Leader @ SaaS -> **Task:** Manual personal outreach from Pavan.
        *   **Tier 2 (Maybe):** Agency/Service -> **Task:** Send "Partner" email.
        *   **Tier 3 (No Fit):** Student/Random -> **Task:** Auto-nurture only.

---

## 5. Execution Routine (How we use it)

**Daily Morning Routine (20 Mins):**
1.  **Check Slack:** Review the "Signals" channel.
2.  **Triage:**
    *   Found 5 Competitor Comments -> **3 Qualified.**
    *   Found 2 "Hiring SDR" posts -> **1 Qualified.**
3.  **Approve Briefings:** Click "Generate Briefing" for the 4 qualified leads.
4.  **Outreach:** Read the 1-page briefing -> Send LinkedIn connection request with the generated hook.

**Weekly Review:**
*   Which signal (Competitor vs. Hiring) produced the most replies?
*   Tune the keywords/filters accordingly.
