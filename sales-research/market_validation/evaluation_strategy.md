# Glial Evaluation Phase Strategy: "The Concierge Pilot"

**Current Phase:** Market Validation / Alpha
**Goal:** Prove that Glial's "Intel" leads to higher reply rates than standard outreach.
**Duration:** 2 Weeks.

Since we are "evaluating," we should **NOT** focus on scale yet. We should focus on **Intensity** and **Quality**.

---

## 1. The Strategy: "Concierge Implementation"
Do not ask partners or users to "log in and figure it out." The UX is likely not ready for self-serve.
Instead, we sell the **Result**, not the Software.

**The Offer:** "We will run the Glial engine for you on 10 accounts and send you the briefing."

### Why this approach?
*   **Low Friction:** Partner/User doesn't need to learn a new tool.
*   **High Control:** We can manually check the AI's output before sending it, ensuring 100% quality (and hiding any bugs).
*   **Instant Value:** They get the "Intel" immediately.

---

## 2. Evaluation Workstreams

### Stream A: Internal Dogfooding (The "First 50")
**Goal:** Generate 5 Meetings for Glial using Glial.
*   **Target:** 50 High-Value Prospects (Founders/VPs).
*   **Method:**
    1.  Use Glial to generate the hooked line.
    2.  Send the email manually.
    3.  **Audit:** Rate every single Glial output on a 1-5 scale (Did it find the right news? Was the hook cringe?).
*   **Success Metric:** >15% Reply Rate. (Standard cold email is <2%).

### Stream B: Partner "Teaser" Pilots
**Goal:** Get 3 Partners to trust us with ONE client each.
*   **Action:**
    *   Don't ask them to resell it yet.
    *   Ask: "Give me your toughest active deal or a dream client you can't break into."
    *   Run Glial on that *one* account.
    *   Send the Partner the **Executive Briefing**.
*   **The "Aha" Moment:** When the Partner reads the briefing and says, "Whoa, I didn't know they were launching a new product." -> **Validation Complete.**

---

## 3. What to Measure (KPIs)

During this evaluation, ignore "User Growth" or "Revenue." Focus on **Utility**:

1.  **Signal Accuracy:**
    *   Did Glial find a *real* signal (e.g., hiring SDRs), or was it a hallucination?
    *   *Target:* 90% Accuracy.

2.  **Hook Relevance:**
    *   Is the generated opening line better than a generic "Hi [Name]"?
    *   *Test:* A/B test Glial Hook vs. Generic Hook on 20 leads.

3.  **Human intervention Time:**
    *   How long did it take us to fix the AI's output?
    *   *Goal:* < 2 minutes per lead. (If it takes 20 mins, we aren't automating anything).

---

## 4. The "Go/No-Go" Decision
At the end of 2 weeks, we decide:

*   **GO (Scale):** If reply rates are >10% AND partners are asking for more briefings. -> *Build Self-Serve UI.*
*   **ITERATE (Pivot):** If reply rates are low (<5%). -> *Change the Signal (maybe "Hiring" isn't the right trigger).*
*   **STOP:** If the AI output constantly requires complete rewriting. -> *Fix the Backend.*
