# **Execution Philosophy: Dogfooding First**

The smartest way to launch Glial is to USE Glial to find your first prospects. This is called 'dogfooding' and it has three massive advantages:

**1\. Instant Credibility**

When you reach out saying 'I noticed you commented on Apollo\\'s post about pricing,' the prospect knows you\\'re not blasting generic emails. You have actual signal.

**2\. Product Validation**

You\\'ll immediately see if Glial finds high-quality signals or garbage. If your own monitoring doesn\\'t produce good leads, you know you need to fix the product before selling it.

**3\. Proof in the Demo**

During demos, you can say: 'Here\\'s how I found YOU' and show them their own LinkedIn activity in the system. That\\'s far more compelling than a hypothetical example.

Not every signal is worth pursuing. Use these qualification criteria:

| ✓ QUALIFY | ✗ SKIP |
| :---- | :---- |
| Right job title (VP+) | Individual contributor or junior sales role |
