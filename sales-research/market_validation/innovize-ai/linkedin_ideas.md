### 1. The "Demand Hijacker" Strategy
*   **The Hook:** "Your competitors are paying for ads to attract leads. You're going to find them in their comments for free."
*   **The Idea:** Showcase the **Competitor Comment Monitoring** feature. Explain how when someone asks a competitor "Does this integrate with X?" or "How much is this?", they are signaling high intent.
*   **Natural CTA:** "How are you currently tracking high-intent signals outside of paid ads?"

### 2. The "X-Ray" Vision Hack
*   **The Hook:** "How to bypass LinkedIn search limits and find every CTO in London using Google Dorks."
*   **The Idea:** Explain the automated Google Dorking logic (`site:linkedin.com/in`) that the tool uses to find prospects.
