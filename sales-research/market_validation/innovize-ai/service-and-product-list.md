## **1. Product: Glial Revenue Intelligence System**
*The autonomous "Sales Brain" and intelligence infrastructure for revenue teams.*

### **Core Modules & Functionality**
*   **Command Center (Identify & Intercept)**: A dedicated frontend to steer the research engine. Identifies prospects from competitor intercepts, keyword intelligence, and Apollo integrations.
*   **Primary Evaluation (Instant Sifting)**: Automated analysis of LinkedIn profiles and headlines to instantly tag leads as *Potential Fit*, *Decision Maker*, or *Competitor*.
*   **Deep Analysis (Decision Briefings)**: Executive-grade reports delivered directly to Slack or CRM. Includes trigger event analysis ("Why Now"), AI-driven pain hypotheses, and hyper-personalized engagement plans.
*   **Continuous Signal Loop (Always-Monitoring)**: Ongoing tracking of account signals (emails, lead magnet downloads, competitor comments). Re-runs analysis automatically as context shifts.

### **Pricing & Model**
*   **Deployment Model**: Infrastructure Ownership (PaaS). Clients own the engine and pay raw API costs with zero markup.
*   **Initial Deployment**: **$3.5K – $5K** (One-time). includes 2-3 week build, playbook ingestion, and core integrations.
