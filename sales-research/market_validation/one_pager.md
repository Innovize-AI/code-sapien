# Glial Revenue Intelligence: Surgical Precision in Sales Outreach

Glial is an advanced Revenue Intelligence Platform designed to help B2B sales teams eliminate the "generic outreach" grind and identify high-intent buying signals with surgical precision.

## The Problem
Sales teams spend up to 20 hours a week on manual lead research, only to produce generic outreach that gets ignored.

## Our Solution: Glial Revenue Intelligence
Glial automates deep prospect research by synthesizing LinkedIn signals, company news, and hiring data into actionable intelligence.

### Key Features
- **Competitor Comment Tracking**: Automatically identify and qualify prospects interacting with your competitors on social media.
- **Strategic Personalization**: Generate hyper-personalized outreach hooks based on real-time buying signals (e.g., specific hiring needs or recent funding).
- **Executive Synthesis**: A one-page "Executive Briefing" for every lead, connecting news and trends into a "Why Now" narrative.
- **High-Stakes Discovery**: Automated detection of organizational pain points (Growth Blockers, Operational Inefficiencies).
