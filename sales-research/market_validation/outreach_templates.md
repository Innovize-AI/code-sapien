# Outreach Templates for Glial Market Validation

These templates are designed for the **Primary Persona** (Founders/Sales Leaders at Seed-Series B Startups). They focus on a "Consultative/Discovery" approach rather than a hard sell.

## Strategy A: The "Competitor Signal" Hook
*Target: Profiles identified commenting on competitor posts.*

### LinkedIn Request (Under 280 chars)
> Hi {{Name}}, noticed your comment on {{Competitor}}'s post about {{Topic}}. I'm working on something that automates the research you mentioned. Would love to get your feedback on a 10-min call—no sales pitch, just building for people like you. Best, {{Your Name}}.

### Follow-up Email (The "Value Gap" Angle)
**Subject:** Tactical research on {{Company}}

Hi {{Name}},

Following up on my LinkedIn note.

I'm building Glial (revenue intelligence) specifically for startups that are tired of the "generic outreach" grind. 

I actually ran {{Company}} through our engine. It identified two specific buying signals for you this week:
1. {{Signal 1: e.g., Hiring for X}}
2. {{Signal 2: e.g., Recent news about Y}}

Most tools stop at data; we build the narrative for you.

Can I steal 10 minutes next Tuesday to show you how this could save your team 20 hours a week? I'm in the early validation phase and your perspective as a {{Title}} at {{Company}} would be huge.

Best,

{{Your Name}}

## Strategy B: The "Revenue Operations" Hook
*Target: RevOps or Head of Sales focused on efficiency.*

### LinkedIn Request
> Hi {{Name}}, came across your profile and saw you're leading {{Department}} at {{Company}}. I'm validating a new platform that automates the deep research process for sales teams (saving about 30 mins per lead). Would you be open to a quick chat?

**Subject:** 4x more efficient research for {{Company}} sales?

Hi {{Name}},

As you scale, the biggest bottleneck is usually the time reps spend "getting smart" on a lead before reaching out.

We're validating a tool that cuts that research time to zero by synthesizing LinkedIn signals, news, and hiring data into a one-page "Executive Synthesis."

Our early testers are seeing a 4x boost in productivity.

Would you be open to a 10-minute feedback session? I'd love to show you the "Automated Pain Point Discovery" we've built.

Best,

{{Your Name}}
