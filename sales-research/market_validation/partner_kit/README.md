# Glial Partner Program Kit

Welcome to the Glial Partner Program. This kit is designed to give you everything you need to introduce your network to the **Glial Revenue Intelligence System** and drive high-quality referrals.

## 📂 What's Inside This Kit?

1.  **[Partner One-Pager](partner_one_pager.md)**
    *   **What it is:** A clean, high-impact PDF-ready document explaining *what* Glial is and *why* your clients need it.
    *   **How to use:** Send this as an attachment or link after you've piqued their interest.

2.  **[Email & Message Templates](partner_email_templates.md)**
    *   **What it is:** Ready-to-use copy for emails, LinkedIn DMs, and texts.
    *   **How to use:** Copy, paste, customize slightly, and send. Focuses on "warm introductions" rather than cold sales.

3.  **[ICP & Qualification Guide](icp_qualification_guide.md)**
    *   **What it is:** A cheat sheet to help you identify *who* is a perfect fit for Glial.
    *   **How to use:** Review this before scrolling through your LinkedIn or contacts list to spot the best opportunities.

## 🚀 How the Process Works

1.  **Identify:** Spot a contact who matches the [ICP](icp_qualification_guide.md) (e.g., complaining about "spammy outreach" or "hiring SDRs").
2.  **Pique Interest:** Send a "warm intro" message using one of the [templates](partner_email_templates.md).
3.  **Handover:** Once they say "Sure, I'm open to chatting," introduce them to us via email (cc: `pavan.kumar@the10xacademy.com`).
4.  **We Take Over:** We'll handle the demo, closing, and onboarding. You get updates on the deal status.
