# ICP & Qualification Guide

Use this "Cheat Sheet" to quickly identify if a contact in your network is a good fit for Glial.

## 🎯 The Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
**"Who buys this?"**

### 1. The Persona
*   **Founders (Seed to Series A):** They are still selling but are exhausted. They need leverage.
*   **VP of Sales / Head of Sales:** They have a small team (3-10 reps) and are obsessed with "efficiency" and "pipeline."
*   **RevOps Leaders:** They love tools and automation. They are the easiest to convince about the *tech*.

### 2. The Company Stage
*   **B2B Tech / SaaS:** They understand the value of software.
*   **High Ticket ($10k+ ACV):** If they sell a $50 product, they don't need deep research. They need volume. Glial is for **strategic, high-value sales**.
*   **Team Size:** 3-20 Sales Reps. (Too small? They might not have budget. Too big? Procurement takes forever. The "Scale-Up" phase is the sweet spot).

---

## 🚦 Qualification Signals

### ✅ GREEN LIGHTS (Go for it!)
*   **"We have too many leads and can't call them all."** -> Perfect. Glial auto-qualifies inbound leads so they only talk to the best ones.
*   **"We are hiring SDRs."** -> Great time to pitch automation instead of headcount.
*   **"Our reply rates are tanking."** -> They need better quality/personalization (Glial's specialty).
*   **"My team spends too much time on Admin."** -> Glial removes the research admin work.
*   **"We use Apollo/ZoomInfo but the data is cold."** -> They understand the problem with static data.

### 🚩 RED FLAGS (Walk away)
*   **"We just need 10,000 leads for a blast."** -> Glial is not a spam cannon. They want a cheap lead list.
*   **"We sell to consumers (B2C)."** -> Glial is optimized for B2B LinkedIn signals.
*   **"We have zero budget for tools."** -> Glial is a premium infrastructure play, not a $20/mo plugin.
*   **"We don't do outbound."** -> If they are 100% Inbound/PLG, they might not need this yet.

---

## ❓ The "3 Magic Questions"
If you're chatting with someone, ask these 3 questions to see if they're a fit:

1.  **"How much time does your team spend researching a prospect before they actually send an email?"**
    *   *Answer > 15 mins?* -> **PERFECT MATCH.**

2.  **"Are you guys focused on volume (spam) or quality (strategic) right now?"**
    *   *Answer: "Quality/Strategic"* -> **PERFECT MATCH.**

3.  **"If you could clone your best sales rep's brain, would you?"**
    *   *Answer: "Yes"* -> **PERFECT MATCH.** (That's literally what Glial does).
