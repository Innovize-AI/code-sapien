# Glial Partner Showcase Demo Script

**Goal:** Demonstrate Glial as a "Private Asset" that automates high-fidelity sales research.
**Time:** 5-7 Minutes

---

## 1. The Setup (1 Minute)
*   **Context:** "Most partners use tools like Apollo or Clay. Those are great, but they are *rented tools*. Glial is different."
*   **The Hook:** "Glial is a **Private Revenue Engine**. You build it once, own the infrastructure, and pay zero markup on data."

## 2. The Command Center (Autopilot) (2 Minutes)
*   **Action:** Open the **Legacy Dashboard** (or Main Interface).
*   **Narrative:** "This is the Command Center. Unlike a standard CRM, this is an active listening post."
*   **Show:**
    *   **Competitor Intercepts:** "Here, we are tracking [Competitor Name]. Anyone commenting on their posts is captured every day or what ever frequency you set ."
    *   **Keyword Monitor:** "We're also listening for keywords like 'SDR Burnout' or 'Q4 Planning'."
*   **Key Point:** "This runs 24/7 in the background. The 'Autopilot' engine is doing the work of a junior analyst."

## 3. The "Brain" at Work (Deep Analysis) (2 Minutes)
*   **Action:** Click on a specific Lead Profile (ensure one is pre-loaded).
*   **Narrative:** "This isn't just data enrichment. This is **Strategic Reasoning**."
*   **Show:**
    *   **Fit Score:** "The **Lead Scoring Agent** rated this 85/100. Why? Because they are hiring for Sales Ops (Growth Signal)."
    *   **The Strategy:** "The **Strategy Agent** ingested our 'Enterprise Playbook'. It suggests we pitch the 'Private Asset' angle because this is a CTO."
    *   **The Output:** "It drafted this exact email hook. No templates. Pure context."

## 4. The "Lazarus" Effect (HubSpot Sync) (1 Minute)
*   **Action:** Briefly show the Settings/Integrations tab.
*   **Narrative:** "We also connect to HubSpot. But we don't just push leads."
*   **Feature:** "We monitor **Lost Deals**. If a prospect who said 'No' due to budget suddenly gets funding, Glial wakes up that deal and alerts the rep."

## 5. Closing & Handoff (1 Minute)
*   **Action:** Show Slack (or a screenshot of a Slack alert).
*   **Narrative:** "The rep doesn't even need to log in here. They get a ping in Slack: 'New Hiring Signal on [Account]'. They click 'Approve', and the outreach starts."
*   **The Ask:** "For your clients, we offer a **Priority Deployment**. They get the engine in 2 weeks, skipping our public waitlist."

---

**Preparation Checklist:**
*   [ ] Ensure Backend is running (`uvicorn main:app`).
*   [ ] Ensure Frontend is running (`npm run dev`).
*   [ ] Have 1-2 "Perfect Fit" leads pre-analyzed in the dashboard.
*   [ ] Have a "Competitor" lead visible to show the interception capability.
