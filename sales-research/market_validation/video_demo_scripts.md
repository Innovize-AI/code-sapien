# Sales-Research: Video Demo Scripts (The Glial Series)

This document contains modular scripts for recording feature-specific video demos of the Glial (sales-research) platform. Each module is designed to be a standalone 1-2 minute video.

---

## Module 1: The Command Center (Autopilot Tracking)
**Goal:** Show how Glial acts as a 24/7 "Active Listening Post" to intercept high-intent leads.

### Scene Setup
- **Screen:** Dashboard / Command Center.
- **State:** Backend and Frontend running. Ensure the "Identified Profiles" or "Activities" board has fresh data from a competitor or keyword.

### Script
1.  **The Hook (0:00-0:15):** 
    "Most sales teams manually grind through LinkedIn to find leads. Glial turns that into Autopilot. This is our Command Center."
2.  **The Action (0:15-0:45):** 
    *(Point to Competitor Intercepts)* 
    "We’re currently tracking [Competitor Name]. The moment someone engages with their post, Glial intercepts them. Here, you see [Lead Name]—they were just captured 10 minutes ago because they commented on a rival's scalability issue."
3.  **The Context (0:45-1:10):** 
    "It’s not just names. It’s keywords. We’re listening for 'SDR burnout' or 'Q4 Planning' across the web. These aren't just leads; they're *intent signals*."
4.  **The Outro (1:10-1:20):** 
    "It runs 24/7 while you sleep. By Monday morning, your queue is full of high-intent buyers."

3.  **The Revival (0:40-1:10):**
